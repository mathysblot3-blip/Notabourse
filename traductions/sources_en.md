Each reference gives the authors, year, title, journal in italics, volume (issue), pages and the DOI link to the
publisher's official page (access to the text may be paid). References were checked on 20 October 2026 against
Crossref, the database where publishers register this information, or otherwise against the document itself.

### 5. Data

**Used by the website** (at each search, and every Saturday for the S&P 500 reference)

- Yahoo Finance, through the unofficial Python library yfinance: prices, annual and quarterly accounts, market
  capitalisation, industry.
- U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), EDGAR: industry codes (SIC) and filing dates of the accounts.
- Wikipedia contributors. "[List of S&P 500 companies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_S%26P_500_companies)",
  English Wikipedia, licence [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/deed.en): current members
  of the index.
- Kenneth R. French, [Data Library](https://mba.tuck.dartmouth.edu/pages/faculty/ken.french/data_library.html):
  definition of the 12 sectors by SIC code.

**Used during the research** (2010 to 2026)

- U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC). *Financial Statement Data Sets* (2009 to 2026).
- Historical composition of the S&P 500: GitHub repository [fja05680/sp500](https://github.com/fja05680/sp500) (MIT
  licence) and Wikipedia contributors, "[Historical components of the S&P 500](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Historical_components_of_the_S%26P_500)"
  (CC BY-SA 4.0 licence).
- Yahoo Finance and Tiingo: prices adjusted for dividends and splits.
- S&P 500 Total Return (Yahoo Finance, ticker ^SP500TR); MSCI World Net Return in US dollars (MSCI file downloaded by
  hand).
- Kenneth R. French, [Data Library](https://mba.tuck.dartmouth.edu/pages/faculty/ken.french/data_library.html): Fama and
  French five factors, and momentum.
- Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System (US). *3-Month Treasury Bill Secondary Market Rate, Discount Basis*
  [TB3MS]. Retrieved from [FRED](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/TB3MS), Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis.
- European Central Bank, [ECB Data Portal](https://data.ecb.europa.eu/data/datasets/EXR/EXR.D.USD.EUR.SP00.A): euro to
  US dollar reference exchange rate (appendix in euros).
- Aswath Damodaran, [NYU Stern](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/): industry multiples by region (January 2026
  update), consulted during the research.
- Jensen, T. I., Kelly, B. and Pedersen, L. H. *Global Factor Data* ([jkpfactors.com](https://jkpfactors.com), CC
  BY-NC 4.0 licence), consulted during the research.
